Experiments from Start to End

Local question answering on long and educational videos · Sayed Ilham Azhar Harun · project local-surf · October 2026 · one 16 GB GPU, small frozen open models

For every experiment: first the structure of the framework that was tested (a flow diagram, read left to right), then the performance table. Section 10 is the full grid of every framework version, with every answer model and with or without speech text.

How to read this document

Accuracyshare of questions answered correctly (the final score). Every percentage is accuracy unless the table says otherwise.
Evidence hitshare of questions where the chosen pictures include the moment human annotators marked as showing the answer (CG-Bench only). It measures looking in the right place, not answering.
Dev questionsused during the research to choose settings. Numbers on dev guided decisions.
Test questionslocked away until the end and used once. Test numbers are the trustworthy results.
Confirmedthe 95% confidence interval of a difference does not include zero (bootstrap over videos).
Answer modelthe frozen vision-language model that reads the pictures and answers: Qwen3-VL-2B (small) or Qwen3-VL-4B (larger). Never trained by us.
Speech textup to 600 transcript words given to the answer model next to the pictures. “No speech text”: the transcript is still used to find pictures, but no words are shown to the answer model.
Head Aour trained speech-path model: decides where to look from the spoken words.
Head Bour trained picker: decides which 4 candidate pictures to show the answer model.
DatasetWhat it isUsed for
LongVideoBenchpublic benchmark (NeurIPS 2024): 3,763 videos, multiple choice, subtitles; we used 440 validation questionsv1
MIT lecture questions (self-made)197 multiple-choice questions generated by a larger model from 20 MIT OpenCourseWare lectures, machine-checked; answer-position bias found and correctedv2
EduVidQApublic lecture benchmark (EMNLP 2025), open-ended answersv3 check only
Video-MMMUpublic lecture benchmark (2025): 300 university lecture videos, 10-option multiple choice. Perception = read or see what is on the slide; Comprehension = understand what was taughtv3 (lectures)
CG-Benchpublic long-video benchmark (2024): videos of 10–80 min, human-marked answer seconds; we used 51 English-subtitled videos (train 37 / dev 8 / test 6)v3 (long everyday videos), head training
