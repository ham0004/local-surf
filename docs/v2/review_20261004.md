# Framework 2 review — 2026-10-04

The two-path architecture is retained. Current evidence does not establish a novel learned selector superior to the strongest simple baseline.

## Completed reranker experiment

`scripts/v2_residual_probe.py` trains tiny pairwise ridge residuals around MobileCLIP with nested video CV and existing frozen features. Base has 17 coefficients; the retained-transcript variant has 27. It exports full-pool out-of-fold selections. `scripts/v2_residual_eval.py` evaluates those sets, defaults to cache-only and supports explicit bounded local inference.

Actual four-frame QA, same 197 MIT questions/20 lectures, fixed original pools/transcript/Qwen3-VL-2B:

| Arm | Correct | Accuracy |
|---|---:|---:|
| MobileCLIP | 68 | 34.52% |
| MMR | 71 | 36.04% |
| Zero-shot relevance | 76 | 38.58% |
| Residual base | 74 | 37.56% |
| Residual with retained text | 74 | 37.56% |

Base minus CLIP: +3.05 points, 95% video-bootstrap CI -1.01 to +7.30. Base minus relevance: -1.02 points, CI -5.85 to +3.54. No reliable advantage; OCR still costs time. Keep new models experimental. The CPU ranking proxy is not final QA accuracy.

Evaluation completed with 225 fresh calls, 411.3 seconds inside answer calls, 420.7 seconds total wall time, and no missing questions. Models were local; no downloads or backbone training.

Reproduced in this repository: `reports/v2_review/residual_probe_*.json` (probe, identical metrics on rerun) and `reports/v2_review/residual_qa/` (four-frame QA rows, summary and the fresh-call cache manifest). Historical caches/results are preserved.

## Fixes and next test

- Opt-in `scan_policy="balanced"` retains the 24-position cap. Proposed timestamps cover 711/711 retrieved windows versus 273/711 historically; 178/197 questions previously omitted a window. This is coverage only, not QA improvement. Test in a fresh run directory.
- Speech-feature masking now removes Head A scores/path flags too. The pool remains transcript retrieved.
- Head A evaluation follows its label-generation end-of-line convention for Path A.
- No-OCR heads pay for nearby-text embeddings in composed latency. New pools time OCR and nearby-text batches separately; old combined times cannot be split exactly.
- `CachedTeacher` rejects free-text tasks until an explicit scorer exists.
- The 132 lost positive gains comprise 67 redundant successes, 3 both-alone-correct/combined-wrong cases and 62 frame-correct/transcript-and-combined-wrong cases. Do not call them all redundant.

Use [NExT-GQA](https://github.com/doc-doc/NExT-GQA) for MC QA training and temporal-grounding diagnostics. Temporal spans are released for validation/test; do not train on them. Declare an ASR extension separately.

Training a selector, pairwise utility learning and balanced coverage are not inherently novel. Check TSPO, ReFoCUS, FrameOracle and contextual selection work; require an accuracy/cost advantage on independent videos.
