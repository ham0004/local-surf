# Benchmark survey for v3 (checked 2026-10-09, primary sources only)

Purpose: choose a credible benchmark on which a frame-selection method *can* show an effect, after
EduVidQA's synthetic training split failed the frame-dependence gate. Every fact below was read from
the paper or the official dataset page linked in the last column; numbers not found there are marked
"not stated".

## Candidates

## EduVidQA: what is verified, what is measured, what is not claimed

Verified from the paper (Sec. 3.2, App. C.3.3): the synthetic questions and reference answers were
generated by GPT-4o from NPTEL's manually written transcripts, not from frames; questions answerable
without context were filtered out; the timestamps have ~35 s average error; the authors note that the
context of many visually-worded questions can be obtained from the transcript.

Measured by us (training split only; the 269 expert questions are untouched):
1. **Frames do not improve FactQA** for Qwen3-VL-2B (evenly placed, reference-frame and MobileCLIP-selected
   frames) or for Qwen3-VL-4B (MobileCLIP-selected); on English transcripts accuracy is unchanged and
   coverage drops ~10 points (`docs/v3/gate_g1_report.md`).
2. **The transcript window supports about half of each reference.** With the paper's FactQA prompt and
   Nemotron-3 Ultra as judge, 49% of reference claims are supported by the question's own ±120 s
   transcript vs 0.6% by another video's transcript (45 questions, 14 videos); 53% of questions have at
   least half their reference supported, 24% at least 80% (`reports/v3_gate/transcript_support.json`).
3. **Data fault on our side:** 10 of 87 downloaded transcripts (6 of the 20 gate videos) are Tamil caption
   tracks labelled "en"; they are now excluded by a script check.

Not claimed: that EduVidQA's videos carry no visual information; that the expert-written real questions
behave like the synthetic ones; that the half of each reference not found in the transcript is in the
frames (not measured). Metric caveat: FactQA compares answers with transcript-derived references, so
information visible only in frames and absent from the reference cannot earn credit.

## Recommendation

1. **CG-Bench as the primary development benchmark.** Long videos (10–80 min), open-ended and MCQ,
   subtitles, frames demonstrably necessary, and human-annotated evidence intervals (1.18 per question,
   ~19 s) that can supervise and evaluate Head A directly. No official train split: a video-disjoint
   train/dev/test split must be declared and reported as a custom protocol.
2. **Video-MMMU as an external lecture test** (test only): its Perception-OCR questions directly test
   whether the right slide was selected.
3. **LongVideoBench** remains a secondary MCQ check (frames +22 points over subtitles for GPT-4o).
4. **EduVidQA**: keep only as an answer-quality evaluation (expert test), not for frame-selection claims.

Before any labelling: run the same cheap gate (transcript only vs transcript + frames) on a CG-Bench
sample, so the frame effect is measured with our answerer, not assumed from GPT-4o numbers.
