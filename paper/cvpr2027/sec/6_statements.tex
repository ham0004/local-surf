\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
The authors conceived the research idea, designed the framework and the experiment plan, directed every experiment and checked all results.
Within this plan, Anthropic's Claude, used through the Claude Code agent, implemented the code, ran the experiments and drafted the text of the paper, which the authors reviewed and edited.
A second, independent model, OpenAI's GPT, double-checked the numbers, statistics and wording against the experiment records and helped search the related work.
The lecture test questions were generated by the Nemotron-3.5-Super-VL 120B model (\cref{sec:data}), which is also one of the answer models, and the two model reviews used for agreement statistics in \cref{sec:human} were produced by Claude and GPT.
The authors take full responsibility for the content of the paper.
