\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
The authors conceived the research idea, designed the framework and the experiment plan, directed every experiment and checked all results.
Within this plan, Anthropic's Claude, used through the Claude Code agent, implemented the code, ran the experiments, helped search the related work and drafted the text of the paper, which the authors reviewed and edited.
A second model, OpenAI's GPT, used through the Codex app as a separate AI auditor, double-checked the numbers, statistics and wording against the experiment records and helped search the related work.
The model versions behind these two tools changed during the project and were not recorded for every stage, so we name the tools and model families only.
The lecture questions for training, development and test were generated by the Nemotron-3.5-Super-VL 120B model (\cref{sec:data}), which is also one of the answer models.
The two model reviews of the sampled questions used for agreement statistics in \cref{sec:human} were produced by Claude and GPT; they are AI reviews, not human judgments.
The Qwen3-VL answer models and the 120B answer model are objects of study, not writing aids.
The authors take full responsibility for the content of the paper.
