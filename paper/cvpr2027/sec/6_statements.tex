\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
Large language models were used in this work as follows.
\emph{Research data:} the lecture test questions were generated by the Nemotron-3.5-Super-VL 120B model (\cref{sec:data}), which also served as one of the answer models.
\emph{Code and experiments:} Anthropic's Claude, used through the Claude Code agent, wrote most of the experiment code, ran experiments under the authors' direction and drafted the analysis reports.
\emph{Auditing and related work:} an OpenAI GPT model acted as an independent auditor that checked numbers, statistics and wording against the experiment records and searched for related work; the two model reviews used for agreement statistics in \cref{sec:human} were produced by these two models.
\emph{Writing:} Claude drafted the text of all sections; the authors revised it, and the GPT auditor checked every claim containing a number, comparison or contribution against the records.
The authors take full responsibility for the content of the paper.
